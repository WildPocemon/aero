# Credits and licensing

## Photography

Every photograph in these sites was downloaded from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com),
resized and re-compressed locally, and committed into the relevant site's
`assets/img/` directory. Nothing is hotlinked at runtime.

Photos on Unsplash are covered by the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), which permits free commercial
and non-commercial use without permission or attribution. Attribution is not
required, but the source of each file is recorded below so images can be traced,
re-downloaded at a different size, or replaced.

**Before launch**, consider swapping these for photographs of your own vans,
technicians and completed work. Stock imagery is fine as a starting point, but
a real crew outside a real house converts better than any stock photo — and it
avoids the risk of a competitor in the same market using the same picture.

Note that none of these photographs depict the business the sites advertise. Do
not caption them in a way that implies otherwise.

## Icons and logos

Every icon is inline SVG written for this repository. The seven company logos
and favicons in `sites/*/assets/logo.svg` and `favicon.svg` were drawn for these
sites and carry the `<name>` placeholder in their wordmark — update the text in
the SVG (or run `rename.py`, which rewrites SVG files too) along with the rest
of the branding.

No third-party or manufacturer logos are reproduced anywhere in this repository.
Manufacturer names appear only as plain text in the "brands we service" list on
`appliance-01-bluecrest`.
