FAQ

Straight answers

How fast can you get here?
Ninety minutes is our county-wide average during business hours. Emergency calls — no cooling in a heat wave, no heat with an infant or elderly resident at home, or anything with a gas smell — are dispatched immediately, day or night.
What does a diagnostic cost?
$89 for a full system diagnostic, and it's waived entirely when you approve the repair. You'll have a flat, written price for the fix before any work begins.
Should I repair or replace my system?
Rough rule: if your system is over twelve years old and the repair costs more than a third of a replacement, replacing usually wins on running costs. Under that, repair almost always makes sense. We'll give you the numbers for both and let you decide.
Do you handle refrigerant work?
Yes. Our technicians are EPA Section 608 certified, and we work with R-410A and the newer low-GWP refrigerants. A leak gets found and repaired — we won't just top up a system and call it fixed.
Do you offer maintenance plans?
Yes — two visits a year, one before cooling season and one before heating season, with priority scheduling and a discount on any repairs. It's the cheapest way to avoid an August emergency call.
