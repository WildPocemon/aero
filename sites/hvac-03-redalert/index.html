Three things to check before we arrive

Sometimes it isn't a breakdown at all. Two minutes of checking can save you a call-out — and we'd rather tell you that than take your money.

01 — The breaker

Find the HVAC breaker in your panel. If it's tripped, reset it once. If it trips again straight away, stop and call us — that's an electrical fault, not something to keep resetting.

02 — The filter

Pull the return filter out and hold it up to a light. If you can't see light through it, that alone can freeze a coil and stop cooling. Replace it and give the system an hour.

03 — The thermostat

Confirm it's set to COOL or HEAT rather than FAN, that the setpoint is well past room temperature, and that the batteries aren't flat. Blank screens are usually batteries or a tripped float switch.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you really not charge extra in the evening or at weekends?
Correct. The flat prices in the table above apply at every hour we're open, on every day of the year. We pay our late-shift technicians a premium out of our own margin rather than passing it to whoever happens to break down at 8pm on a Saturday.
How fast can you get to me?
Two to four hours is typical, county-wide. Calls that reach us before about 6pm are usually handled the same evening; anything later is booked first thing the next morning. True emergencies are dispatched ahead of routine work.
What counts as an emergency?
No cooling during a heat advisory, no heat when it's below about 50°F, any smell of gas or burning, water actively leaking into the house, or a system tripping breakers repeatedly. If you smell gas, leave the house first and call the gas company, then us. Outside our 7am–9pm hours, book online and you'll be at the top of the next morning's queue.
Will you try to sell me a new system?
Not unless the repair genuinely doesn't add up. Our technicians earn no commission on equipment. When a system really is past saving we'll show you the repair cost, the replacement cost and the running-cost difference, then leave the decision with you.
Do you work on commercial equipment?
Light commercial — rooftop package units, small office splits and similar. Anything larger than about 25 tons is outside what our trucks are set up for, and we'll refer you rather than fumble it.
